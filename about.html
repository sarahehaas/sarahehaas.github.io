About

The question that connects my work:

What does someone need to understand right now — and why?

That question has shaped my work across journalism, digital publishing, mobile and product content, content strategy, content design, and AI systems.

Senior content designer with editorial roots and a systems mindset.

I work where complexity meets the person trying to make sense of it.

I bring editorial rigor, systems thinking, and human judgment to complex digital experiences. Today that work sits at the intersection of content design, AI systems, product strategy, and product clarity.

My background is in journalism, but the throughline is not writing for its own sake. It is finding the angle, asking sharper questions, separating signal from noise, and shaping information so real people can understand what matters.

That instinct has followed me through digital publishing, mobile products, content strategy, content design, and AI systems.
