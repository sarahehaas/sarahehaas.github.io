Building accessibility readiness for a complex browser ecosystem

Before external experts could audit the product, someone had to make the product testable. I mapped the ecosystem, defined the testing environment, and built the operational process that turned accessibility findings into owned work.

Role
Accessibility lead, Content Design
Scope
In-app browser flows and component ecosystem
Contribution
Mapping, scope, vendor enablement, prioritization, remediation
Challenge

The audit could not begin until the system was understandable.

The in-app browser contained many entry points, flows, states, content elements, icons, and account conditions. Vendors could not reliably reach or test those experiences without a structured map and prepared environment.

Approach

I created the ecosystem and operating model for testing.

  • Audited every in-scope flow and cataloged states, text, and icons.
  • Built test accounts and documented navigation paths for vendors.
  • Defined the testing scope and detailed workflows.
  • Handed vendors a structured environment for the formal accessibility audit.
  • Translated returned scores and issues into priorities, owners, and remediation plans.
  • Coordinated fixes and re-audits, then helped shape longer-term accessibility strategy and guardrails.
Impact 1

Made a complex product testable

The ecosystem map and testing operations removed ambiguity for vendors and internal teams. The audit could evaluate real user paths rather than isolated screens.

Impact 2

Turned audit results into product accountability

I prioritized the findings, assigned owners, coordinated remediation, and re-ran audits. The work also exposed where accessibility had to move upstream into components, standards, education, and development practices rather than remain a recurring cleanup exercise.

Reflection

The hard part was not only finding issues. It was building the system that made quality measurable.

Accessibility became actionable once the product, workflows, ownership, and testing conditions were explicit. That is systems design as much as compliance work.

Sarah Haas · Senior Content Designer & Strategist
