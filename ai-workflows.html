I partnered with engineering to design and pilot an AI-assisted workflow that allowed content designers to add net-new UX strings directly into code — while preserving engineering review and human quality control at every step.

Challenge

What the user needed to know vs. what the business needed them to do

Content designers needed a faster way to ship strings without losing quality or review.

The existing workflow created heavy handoff costs between content design and engineering. The business needed speed and scale. The product still needed quality, traceability, accessibility, and human judgment.

workflow diagram / deck image

Answer

Design a human-in-the-loop AI workflow.

I authored the foundational prompt, shaped the workflow, and helped launch an internal skill that made the process accessible across the team. The workflow used AI for speed while preserving review, validation, and engineering oversight.

Impact

Reduced combined content design and engineering time, improved production efficiency, and created a repeatable model for AI-assisted content workflows.
