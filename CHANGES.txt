  placeholder, not a bug.

  captions) sits below the 4.5:1 AA-normal contrast ratio against the
  butter background (it measures ~2.3:1). This was reviewed and
  accepted as-is: these are captions/secondary labels, not primary
  reading text. All primary body copy and link text on the site clears
  4.5:1.

  (see above).
- HXP's and Tabs' featured artifacts are custom-built HTML/CSS diagrams
  and a text step-list, respectively, rather than images, since no
  image assets were supplied for either.
- advertiser-education.html and device-help.html remain unfinished
  placeholder pages, intentionally not linked from navigation, the
  homepage, or the Portfolio grid.
- portfolio.html, autofill-optimization.html, in-app-browser.html remain
  as redirect stubs so old bookmarked links keep working.
- device-help.html has two instances of an unescaped "AT&T" and
  advertiser-education.html has one unescaped "&" in its H1 (should be
  "&amp;"); both are pre-existing, cosmetic-only (browsers render them
  correctly as-is), and out of scope since neither page's content was
  part of this redesign pass.
