Optimizing content-to-code with AI

Content Design handed finalized strings to Engineering for manual implementation. I designed and piloted a human-in-the-loop workflow that let content designers add strings directly to code while Engineering retained technical review, cutting implementation time 91% with zero post-launch corrections.

Building a shared strategy for a complex Autofill ecosystem

As our team prepared to inherit Autofill, a global product and critical revenue driver, much of its history lived as institutional knowledge. I led a cross-functional audit that synthesized research, performance data, competitive analysis, and cross-platform gaps into five strategic opportunities and a shared source of truth for product prioritization.

Closing a critical gap in the browsing journey

People who left Meta’s in-app browser couldn’t easily re-engage with what they had been exploring or pick up where they left off. In collaboration with Product and Design, I defined the design principles, naming, value proposition, and first-use experience for a 0→1 Tabs product that secured roadmap investment, launched in Facebook’s in-app browser, and beat its engagement goal by 46%.

Building an accessibility operating model for EAA readiness

With the browser prioritized for European Accessibility Act readiness, the team needed a reliable baseline across a complex consumer-product ecosystem. I identified priority flows, partnered with Product Design and Engineering to document reproducible test paths, and delivered the scope and instructions vendors used to assess and triage them. That approach became a repeatable model across products.

Prioritizing trust and user needs in Autofill consent

Research showed that people prioritized clear signals of value, trust, control, and security when deciding whether to save payment information. As the team explored ways to improve plateauing adoption, I restructured the experience around those needs, reducing the full disclosure 46% and creating a reusable baseline that preserved legal and privacy requirements.
